## 🛠 Step 1. System Preparation

Since our software is a true External application operating at the kernel level (Kernel-mode) and rendering visuals over the game, antivirus software will trigger false positives and block the process.

**1.1. Disable your antivirus**

Completely disable **Windows Defender** and any third-party antivirus software (Avast, Kaspersky, Malwarebytes, etc.).

📖 If you need help doing this manually, check out this guide: [Windows Central Guide](https://www.windowscentral.com/how-permanently-disable-windows-defender-windows-10)

**1.2. Prevent protection from re-enabling itself**

To prevent Windows from automatically turning real-time protection back on during gameplay, we highly recommend using the **Defender Control** utility.

📥 [Download Defender Control on Softportal](https://www.softportal.com/software-43861-defender-control.html)

How to use Defender Control:

1. Extract the downloaded archive (password: `sordum`)
2. Right-click `dControl.exe` → **Run as Administrator**
3. Click **"Disable Windows Defender"**
4. The interface will turn red — protection is fully disabled ✅

**1.3.** Make sure the game you want to play is currently **closed**.

## 🗝 Step 3. Key Activation

> 💡 This step is only required on your **first launch** or when adding a new product. If you already have active subscriptions — the loader will open the Library directly and you can skip this step.

If the loader opens the **"Activate product"** screen:

1. Enter your license key in the format `XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX` — dashes are inserted automatically
2. Or click the **Paste** button — the loader will paste and format the key from your clipboard automatically
3. Click the **Activate** button

After successful activation your product will appear in the library.

> To activate an additional key later, use the **Activate key** button at the bottom of the Library panel.

| 📌 Name | Product name |
| 🟢 Status | Undetected / Detected / On Update |

**4.2.** Click on the product — a details panel will open on the right showing:

**4.3.** Click the **Load** button in the bottom-right corner.

**4.4.** Once initialization is complete, the loader will display **"Please start [game name]"** — the loader window will close automatically. Launch your game.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

---

**❌ The loader window closes instantly upon launch**

Windows Defender (or a third-party antivirus) has deleted or blocked the loader file. Add the loader folder to your antivirus exclusions or completely disable real-time protection.

---

**❌ Loader stuck on "Connecting" / "Maintenance" error**

The loader cannot reach the server. Check that your antivirus and firewall are completely disabled. If the issue persists, the server may be undergoing maintenance — please try again later.

---

**❌ "Access blocked" error**

Your account or HWID has been flagged. Contact support — in the error window you can copy your full HWID using the copy button next to it and send it to our team.

---

**❌ Key activation error**

Double-check the key you entered. Make sure the key has not already been activated on another account.

---

**❌ Computer restarted, but the menu/visuals do not appear in-game**

Make sure you launched the loader **before** launching the game. Also verify your in-game video settings — the game must be in **Borderless Windowed** or **Windowed** mode. The overlay cannot render in Exclusive Fullscreen mode.

---

**❌ Product launch failed ("Product launch failed" screen)**

The error details will be shown in the window. Click **Back to library**, select your product again and try launching once more. If the error repeats — contact support.

---

**❌ "Secure Boot is enabled. Do you want to enter BIOS right now?" window appears**

Secure Boot is enabled on your computer, which is blocking the product from launching. You need to disable it in your BIOS settings. [Detailed instructions](secure_boot.md).
